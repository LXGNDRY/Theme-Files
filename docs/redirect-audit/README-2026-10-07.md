# Redirect audit: legendary-branding.com (2026-10-07)

Scope: all 501 Shopify URL redirects (Online Store > Navigation > URL Redirects), checked against the live catalog and the live site.
Companion file: `redirect-audit-2026-10-07.csv` (one row per redirect: `source`, `target`, `state`, `hops`, `final`, `final_state`, `suggested`, `confidence`). The `target` column is the **current** value, so the CSV doubles as the rollback record for any change made from it.

## Executive summary

- **Only 130 of 501 redirects (26%) land on a live, relevant page.**
- **174 (35%) send visitors to the homepage.** Google treats most of these as soft 404s. They pass no ranking value.
- **135 (27%) point at a dead page:** the product is unlisted, a draft, or deleted, so the redirect ends in a 404.
- **52 (10%) point at a live product through a collection that no longer exists.** They work, but cost an extra hop (Shopify re-redirects to `/products/<handle>`).
- **9 target URL fragments** (`/#seotid...`) left by an SEO app. Servers ignore fragments, so these are homepage redirects.
- **No redirect loops.** 3 two-hop chains, all ending on a dead page.
- **No redirect shadows a live product** (no source is an ACTIVE product handle).

### Specific defects worth knowing about

- **`/pages/ccpa-opt-out` redirects to the homepage.** A live page exists at `/pages/data-sharing-opt-out` ("Your Privacy Choices"). A privacy-choice URL landing on the homepage is a compliance risk; have counsel confirm the right target.
- **`/pages/contact-us` redirects to a `#seotid` fragment.** The live page is `/pages/contact`.
- **Blog redirects into `/blogs/news/`**: that blog does not exist. 3 redirects.
- **Chains (all end dead):** `legendary-butterfly-t-shirt` -> `butterfly-tee` -> `the-savage-butterfly-tshirt`; `men-s-solid-color-...-jogger-set` -> `mens-solid-...` -> `solid-color-...`; `featured-product` -> `featured-products` -> `legendary-select`.
- **`yellow-dot-athletic-long-short` is a valid live handle** (the apparent truncation is real). `simply-goated-athletic-long-shor` is also a real handle, but unlisted.
- **Autoketing leftovers:** `/pages/autoketing-sitemap-*` redirects (7) belong to an app that appears to be gone; `robots.txt` also disallows `/*/pages/html-sitemap`.

## Remediation plan (nothing below has been applied to the store)

| Tier | Change | Rows | Risk | Why safe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retarget collection-prefixed targets to canonical `/products/<handle>` where the product is live | 52 | Very low | Same final page; removes a hop |
| 1 | `neverdy-foam-trucker-hat` -> `/products/the-lb-hat-foam-trucker-hat` | 10 | Low | Currently ends in a 404; replacement is the same product family |
| 1 | `/pages/ccpa-opt-out` -> `/pages/data-sharing-opt-out`; `/pages/contact-us` -> `/pages/contact` | 2 | Low | Live pages exist (confirm CCPA target with counsel) |
| 2 | Remaining dead-target rows (includes the 3 chains and `/llms.txt`): retarget to the closest category collection (`shirts-tops`, `hoodies-jackets`, `accessories-more`) or publish the unlisted products | 125 | Medium | Needs a merchandising decision (see `suggested` column; confidence M/L) |
| 3 | Homepage redirects: delete rows with no relevant current product and no backlinks (404 is the correct answer); retarget the rest to a category | 174 | Medium | Check Search Console "Page with redirect" and backlinks first; legacy products (sandals, jeans, dresses) have no equivalent in the current catalog |
| 3 | Remove Autoketing and `/#seotid` rows | ~16 | Low | App/leftover cleanup |

Tier 1 alone fixes 64 redirects (13%). Tiers 1 and 2 together leave only intentional category fallbacks and the 130 redirects that already work; tier 3 is a deletion/cleanup pass.

## Method and limits

- Redirect list: Shopify Admin GraphQL (`urlRedirects`, all 501). Catalog: `products` (113: ACTIVE / UNLISTED / DRAFT), `collections`, `pages`, `blogs`/`articles`.
- Live checks: HTTP status (no redirect following) for about 100 unique targets and key sources on `www.legendary-branding.com`; Shopify auto-redirects `/collections/<missing>/products/<handle>` to `/products/<handle>`.
- Not available: Search Console and backlink data, so there is no traffic or link-equity weighting per redirect. Tier 2/3 decisions should check those first.
- Product "unlisted" is inferred as not live because the live URL returned 404 for each.
